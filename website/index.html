OUR STORY

The Sweetest Jelly
in India 🇮🇳

Lonawala Jelly is a product of Kesar Bhavani Industries — a passionate confectionery brand bringing you the finest sugar coated soft jelly candies made with real fruit pulp and the finest ingredients.

Our jellies are 100% Pure Vegetarian, contain No Gelatin, and are crafted under strict FSSAI, ISO & Halal certified standards. Every bite is a burst of real fruit flavour wrapped in a satisfying sugar coat.

Available in 17 exciting flavours — from classic Mango and Strawberry to desi favourites like Imli, Paan and Jalebi — there's a Lonawala Jelly for everyone!
